You open your payout summary and something doesn't add up. Same house in Mexico, same
nightly rate, and less money than last year.

You did nothing wrong. The way Airbnb charges changed, and it is worth understanding
calmly, because it can be fixed.

## What changed

Airbnb has two ways of charging its fee. This is how its Help Center explains them for
Mexico:

| | Split fee | Single fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host pays | 4% | 16% |
| Guest pays | 14.1 to 16.5% of the subtotal | Nothing on top |

With the split fee, guests saw a service fee on top of your price. With the single fee,
it all comes from your side and the guest sees one price. Airbnb says the split fee is
being phased out and that the single fee is already mandatory for most hosts. The fee
you see includes VAT where applicable.

## The numbers, without a scare

A house at 2,500 pesos a night, before taxes:

| Per night | Before, split | Now, unchanged | Now, adjusted price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your price | 2,500 | 2,500 | 2,857 |
| What the guest pays | 2,853 to 2,913 | 2,500 | 2,857 |
| What you keep | 2,400 | 2,100 | 2,400 |

If you change nothing, you get 300 pesos less per night. Raise your price to 2,857 and
you keep 2,400 again. And the guest pays about what they paid before, because the fee
they used to see separately is now inside the price.

## What it feels like

It is normal to be annoyed at a smaller payout. What changed is a rule you did not
write, and nobody likes that.

But this is not a reason to worry, it is a reason to decide. Airbnb still does its part
well: putting you in front of travellers who don't know you. What is in your hands is
the price, and where the guests who already know you book.

## What you do decide

**Your price on Airbnb.** To keep the same amount at 16%, divide what you want to
receive by 0.84. For 2,400 pesos, that is 2,857.

**Where your returning guests book.** On your own page there is no platform fee. You pay
what Stripe charges for the card and the Booking Page fee.
[We ran that full sum with Mexican rates here](../what-a-booking-really-costs-in-mexico/).

**Your rules.** Seasons, minimum stay, cancellations. On your page, you write them.

## Worth knowing before you start

**Compare before you raise.** Check how your total price looks next to similar homes in
your area on Airbnb. A reasonable adjustment barely shows; a sharp one does.

**VAT, with your accountant.** Airbnb's fee includes VAT "where applicable". If you
invoice, your accountant will tell you how to credit it.

**Your own page is for the guests who already know you.** Booking Page has a fixed fee
and pays for itself once you have guests who come back.

**Airbnb is still your shop window.** New guests find you there, and that is worth
something.

## Where to start

Open your last Airbnb payout and check which fee you were charged. Then run the sum with
your price: what you want to receive, divided by 0.84. In five minutes you know whether
to adjust.

[The Booking Page calculator](/booking-page/en/) shows what you would keep if your
returning guests booked with you.
