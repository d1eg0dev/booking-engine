There is a family that comes back to your house every December. You know the
kids' names. They message you on WhatsApp before they book.

You built that relationship. It can have its own front door too.

## What Airbnb and Booking do well

Platforms do something very hard to do alone: put you in front of thousands
of travellers who do not know you. That is why they charge. In Mexico, Airbnb's
single host fee is 16%, according to its Help Center.

For a new guest, that deal makes sense. The platform brought them.

The returning guest is a different story. You already won them over with the
clean house, the quick reply, the small touch on arrival. There you do not
need a middleman. You need a place of your own where they can book.

## Your page, your rules

Having your own booking page does not mean leaving Airbnb or Booking. It
means adding a channel that is yours. It works the same for an apartment as
for a villa or a boutique hotel.

**Your rules.** Your cancellation policy, your seasons, your minimum stay,
your prices. You set them and change them when you want.

**Your guest.** You have their email and phone from day one. Next year, you
write to them.

**Your money.** Payment lands straight in your Stripe account.

**Your brand.** The page looks like your house, not one more listing in a
list. And the calendar syncs with Airbnb and Booking, so you stay on both.

## The numbers, plainly

We ran the sum for a house at 2,500 pesos a night, with two four-night stays
a month booked direct instead of through Airbnb.

| How the guest pays | You keep per month, against Airbnb |
| --- | --- |
| Mexican card, in pesos | +734 pesos |
| Foreign card, in pesos | +600 pesos |
| Foreign card, in dollars | +61 pesos |

In Mexico the saving is smaller than in Europe, because Stripe costs more
here: 3.6% plus 3 pesos per charge, with VAT on its fee. Charge in dollars and
another 2% goes on conversion. Booking Page, at 1,490 pesos a month, is
already taken off.

The money is a bonus. What you really gain is that this part of the business
runs on your rules.

## Where this is unfair

**You still need the platform.** If your bookings come from searches on
Airbnb or Booking, stay there. Your own page is for the guests who already
know you.

**The fee is fixed.** Under roughly 13,400 pesos a month in direct bookings,
Booking Page costs you more than it saves.

**The paperwork becomes yours.** On a direct booking, Airbnb withholds no
tax. You charge the VAT, issue the invoice and file it. If you treat Airbnb's
withholding as your final tax today, charging direct can take you out of
that option. Talk to your accountant first.

**Moving people takes work.** The December family will not find your page on
their own. Give them the link when they leave, and put it on your Instagram.

## Where to start

Think of your three most loyal guests. If next year they booked with you, on
your rules, what would change? If you like the answer, start with them.

On [the Booking Page site](/booking-page/en/) you can run the sum with your
own rate and nights.
