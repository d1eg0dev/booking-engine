Questions

What people usually ask us.

Where does the money from my bookings go?

Into your Stripe account, the same day the guest pays. We never receive it at any point. Stripe deducts its own card processing fee, which depends on your country, and pays the rest into your bank on the schedule you choose.

Will it bring me new guests?

Not on its own. Booking Page isn't a platform: it has no search and no traffic of its own. It lets the people who already find you (repeat guests, referrals, people who message you on Instagram) book with you without a middleman. If a platform fills your calendar today, the sensible thing is to stay on both: the platform for new people, your page for the ones who already know you.

Do I need my own domain?

No. Your page goes live on one of our addresses from day one. If you buy your own domain later, we connect it and it's yours: if you ever leave, it stays with you.

I'm already on Airbnb. Do I have to leave?

No. Dates sync both ways through iCal, so bookings never overlap.

Can I cancel?

Whenever you like, by email. The service runs until the end of the period you've already paid for, and we hand over your setup and photos for you to keep.

What happens if I stop paying?

We let you know. After fifteen days the page stops accepting new bookings. Bookings already confirmed aren't touched, because they live in your Stripe and your inbox. We keep your information for sixty days in case you want to come back.
