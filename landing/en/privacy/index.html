Privacy policy

What data we touch, why, and what you can ask us for. In short: almost everything of yours lives in your own accounts, not in ours.

Version 2026-11-05 · In force since 5/11/2026

Who is responsible

WD Studio Agency, legal name and address above. For anything about this policy: hola@wdstudio.agency.

What we collect from you

What you put in the signup form: your name, email, phone, your property's details, and the photos and text you upload. Nothing else. We use no advertising trackers and we sell data to nobody.

What we use it for

To build and run your page, charge your subscription and write to you about the service. We do not send you third-party advertising.

Your guests' data

When someone books on your page, their details go to your spreadsheet and your Stripe account. They are yours: towards your guests, you are the controller. We only operate the tool that moves them, and we use them for nothing else.

Who it is shared with

Nobody who isn't needed to make this work: Stripe processes payments, Google hosts the spreadsheets and the booking engine, and GitHub hosts the pages. Each has its own policy and handles the data to provide us that service and nothing more.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a customer. If you cancel, 60 days more in case you come back, and then it is deleted. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we do.

What you can ask for

To access your data, correct it, take it with you or delete it. Write to hola@wdstudio.agency and we answer within 30 days at most. If you are in the European Union, you can also complain to your country's data protection authority.

Cookies

The public site uses no tracking cookies. We store the language you picked in your browser so we don't ask again. That never leaves your device and cannot identify you.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above and, when the change matters, we tell you by email.
